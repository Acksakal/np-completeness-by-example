Exam scheduler: build, traverse, backtrack

Exams are dots, shared students are edges, time slots are colors. Same algorithm as the C code.

Rows and columns are exams. Every conflict fills two cells, mirror images across the diagonal. While solving, the program only looks at the cells below the diagonal (each exam is compared only with exams placed before it), so those cells keep a grey frame even when they turn purple.
Line = a student takes both exams. Fill color = slot. Blue ring = exam being placed, red ring = no slot fits.
